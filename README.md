## 💔 The Problem Every Crocheteer Faces

Ask any crochet maker, and you will hear the exact same heartbreaking story:

> *"I spent 14 hours crocheting a plush bunny for a customer. I charged ₹450 because I felt guilty asking for more. After subtracting the yarn and safety eyes, I basically earned ₹15 an hour..."*

### The Universal Crocheteer Dilemma:
1. **The Yarn Math Trap**: You buy a 100g skein for ₹280, but only use 65 grams. How much did the yarn actually cost? Most makers either count the full skein or guess too low.
2. **The "Free Labor" Guilt**: Crocheting cannot be replicated by machines — every single loop is made by human hands. Yet crafters feel awkward charging for their hours and minutes.
3. **The Hidden Expense Leak**: Packaging boxes, satin ribbons, polyfill stuffing, thank-you cards, pattern licenses, and hook wear eat away profits unnoticed.
4. **The Dreaded "Walmart Comparison"**: Hearing *"I can buy a beanie for ₹199 at a retail store"* makes artisans doubt their worth.

**Crochet Studio** was built to solve this once and for all. It gives you confidence, crystal-clear pricing math, and instant professional client quotes in seconds.

## ✨ Features Built For Real Crafters

### 1. 🧵 Exact Yarn Gram Calculation
Never guess leftover yarn costs again. Enter:
- **Yarn Used (in grams)** (e.g. 75g)
- **Total Skein Weight (in grams)** (e.g. 100g)
- **Price of Full Yarn** (e.g. ₹260)
- *The app instantly calculates your exact yarn cost down to the penny!*

### 2. ⏱️ Making Time in Hours AND Minutes
Whether a project takes 45 minutes or 18 hours and 30 minutes:
- Accurate decimal conversion (`Hours + Minutes / 60`).
- Quick-add steppers (`+15 min`, `+30 min`, `+1 hour`, `+2 hours`).
- Transparent hourly wage calculation.

### 3. 📦 Packaging & Studio Overheads
Factor in the finishing touches that elevate your brand:
- Mailer boxes, dust bags, thank-you cards, custom care tags.
- Hook depreciation, pattern licenses, and studio electricity.

### 4. 📈 Real-Time Cost Breakdown & Profit Slider
- **Break-Even Cost**: The absolute minimum price below which you are losing money.
- **Fair Retail Price**: Sustainable price with your selected profit margin (10% to 100%).
- **Wholesale Price**: Standard 50% discount formula for craft shops and boutique stockists.
- **Live Visual Progress Bar**: Color-coded proportional breakdown showing where every rupee/dollar goes.

### 5. 🧸 1-Click Project Presets
Pre-loaded with realistic crafter benchmarks:
- 🧸 **Amigurumi Plushie**
- 🧶 **Granny Square Cardigan**
- 👶 **Velvet Baby Blanket**
- 🌸 **Pastel Bucket Hat**
- ☕ **Boho Mug Rug Coaster Set (4x)**

### 6. 💬 One-Tap Customer Quote Generator
Instantly copies a charming, professional quote to your clipboard, formatted with emojis and ready to paste into Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, or Etsy!

### 7. 💾 Local Library (Offline Project Storage)
Save calculations with custom names directly in your browser. Re-load them anytime with one click!

### 8. 📐 Built-in Hook & Yarn Weight Guide
Instant popup reference chart mapping US hook sizes, metric millimeters (mm), and yarn weight categories (Lace 0 to Jumbo 7).

Crochet Studio is a full **Progressive Web App (PWA)** optimized specifically for Android smartphones and tablets!

## 🧮 The Pricing Formula Explained

$$\text{Exact Yarn Cost} = \left(\frac{\text{Yarn Used (g)}}{\text{Total Skein Weight (g)}}\right) \times \text{Price of Full Yarn}$$

$$\text{Labour Cost} = \left(\text{Hours} + \frac{\text{Minutes}}{60}\right) \times \text{Hourly Rate}$$

$$\text{Break-Even Cost} = \text{Exact Yarn Cost} + \text{Other Notions} + \text{Labour Cost} + \text{Packaging} + \text{Overheads}$$

$$\text{Recommended Retail Price} = \text{Break-Even Cost} \times \left(1 + \frac{\text{Desired Profit \%}}{100}\right)$$

$$\text{Artisan Take-Home} = \text{Labour Cost} + \text{Profit Amount}$$
